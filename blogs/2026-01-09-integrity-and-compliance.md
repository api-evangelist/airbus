Integrity and compliance carolina.barre… Fri, 01/09/2026 - 11:37 Integrity and compliance A competitive advantage for long-term business growth A robust ethics and compliance programme Everything Airbus does is guided by its core principles of safety, quality, security, integrity and compliance. These pillars enable us to fulfil our commitments while navigating risks and leveraging opportunities. We embed these interconnected principles into our daily operations to ensure resilience, competitiveness and adherence to the highest possible standards. Our Ethics & Compliance Programme and Culture of Integrity reinforce one another. The programme provides the structural framework, policies, and guidance for responsible conduct worldwide; whereas the culture is nurtured as a living commitment maintained with unwavering focus. This section explains: - Our commitments : The core standards that govern our actions. - Our Ethics & Compliance Programme : The detailed policies guiding our daily interactions, addressing key areas like anti-corruption, responsible lobbying, and third-party due diligence. - Our Culture of Integrity : The way we ensure accountability, including training, and a ‘Speak Up’ and ‘Listen Up’ culture that empowers our people.
